Student Study Guide

Practical advice for planning revision and preparing for exams.

Effective Study Techniques

Review your notes regularly instead of waiting until the day before an exam.

  • Summarize important ideas in your own words.
  • Practise answering questions without looking at your notes.
  • Explain difficult topics to a classmate.

How to Plan a Study Session

Use these steps to make a revision session easier to manage.

  1. Choose one topic to study.
  2. Gather your notes and learning materials.
  3. Study the topic and practise questions.
  4. Review what you learned and identify gaps.

Preparing for Exams

Check your examination timetable early and make a revision plan for each subject.

Get enough rest, arrive on time, and read each question carefully before answering.
