First Aid for a Minor Cut

These instructions are for anyone caring for a person with a minor cut. Follow them to clean and cover the wound safely, and known when to seek medical help.

What you need

  • Clean running water
  • Clean gauze or clean cloth
  • A sterile dressing or adhesive bandage
  • Disponsable gloves, also called PPE

Steps

  1. Wash your hands with soap and clean water. Wear gloves if available.
  2. If the cut is bleeding, apply firm, continuous presure with clean gauze or clean cloth Get urgent medical help if bleeding is severe or does not stop.
  3. Once bleeding is controlled, rinse the cut gently with clean running
  4. Pat the surrounding skin dry with clean gauze. Do not rub the wound
  5. Cover the cut with a sterile dressing or clean adhesive bandage.
  6. Keep the dressing clean and change it if it becomes wet or dirty.

If something goes wrong

Seek urgent medical help if bleeding is severe , the wound is deep, or something is embedded in it.Do not remove an embedded object yourself.If the wound may be serious , do not delay getting help.Clean the wound gently, not roughly.If you are unsure how serious the injury is, contat a healthcare professional.
