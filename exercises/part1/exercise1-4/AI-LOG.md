# AI Log — Exercise 1.4

## 1. What I Asked

I asked AI to help me create a first-aid instruction webpage for treating a minor cut.

## 2. What I Got

I got HTML code with an introduction, a list of supplies, ordered steps, and a section explaining what to do if something goes wrong.

## 3. What I Changed

I used <strong> for important safety warnings, <em> to emphasize words, and <abbr> to explain abbreviations.

## 4. Why

I used <strong> to mark important information, <em> to emphasize words that affect the meaning of the instructions, and <abbr> to provide the full meaning of an abbreviation. I chose these tags to mark up meaning, not just appearance, as required by the exercise.
