How to Open an Ecobank Account

Opening a bank account allows you to keep your money safely and access banking services. Follow these steps to prepare for your visit to Ecobank.

Before You Go

Before visiting the bank, find out what is required to open an account. Think about how you plan to use the account and ask Ecobank about its current requirements.

Documents to Prepare

From my experience, the documents requested included a Tax Identification Number (TIN), a national ID card or passport, and two passport-size photographs. Confirm the current requirements with the bank before visiting.

Choose Your Account Type

Consider how you want to save and access your money. Ask bank staff to explain the available account types and their terms before deciding.

Savings Account

A savings account is intended for keeping money and saving over time. Ask the bank about its fees, interest, and rules for accessing your funds.

Fixed Deposit Account

A fixed deposit account lets you deposit money for an agreed period. Before choosing it, ask about the interest rate, maturity date, and conditions for early withdrawal.

What to Bring

Bring the identification documents, TIN details, and passport-size photographs required for your application. Contact Ecobank first to confirm whether you need any additional documents or an initial deposit.

At the Bank

Tell a member of staff that you want to open an account. Follow their instructions, complete the required forms, and provide the information and documents requested.

Complete the Application

Fill in the application form carefully and check that your personal details are correct before submitting it.

Verify Your Information

Provide the required documents and follow the bank's identity-verification process. Ask the staff if you do not understand any part of the application.

After Opening the Account

Ask the bank how to confirm that your account is active and how to access the services available with your account type.

Getting a Bankbook

Ask whether a bankbook is available for your account and how to obtain it.

Getting an ATM Card

Ask whether you can request an ATM card and how to activate it for withdrawals and other transactions.

Setting Up Mobile Banking

Ask the bank how to register for its mobile banking service and use the app securely.
