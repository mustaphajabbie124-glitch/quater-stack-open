
# AI Log — Exercise 1.2

## What I Asked

I asked ChatGPT to help me plan a public information notice explaining how to open an Ecobank bank account. I wanted to organize the page using the correct HTML heading structure and explain the process to people who were unfamiliar with it.

I also asked for help deciding which headings and subsections to include.

## What I Got

ChatGPT suggested a heading outline with one <h1>for the page title, <h2> elements for the main sections, and <h3> elements for subsections.

It suggested sections about preparing documents, choosing an account type, visiting the bank, completing the application, verifying information, and accessing services after opening the account.

It also helped draft paragraphs explaining the steps.

## What I Changed and Why

I chose opening an Ecobank account as the topic because I had personal experience with the process.

I provided the document requirements I experienced: a TIN, a national ID card or passport, and two passport-size photographs.

I suggested including savings accounts and fixed deposit accounts because customers may need to understand the differences between account types.

I also suggested separate subsections for getting a bankbook, requesting an ATM card, and setting up mobile banking. I wanted the notice to address services customers might need after opening an account.

I reviewed the proposed heading structure and kept the main sections as <h2> headings and their related subsections as <h3> headings. I used wording that advises readers to confirm current requirements with Ecobank because requirements and services may vary.
