
# AI Log — Exercise 1.2

## How I Used AI

I used ChatGPT to help me plan the heading outline for my public information notice about opening an Ecobank bank account.

AI helped me understand the correct use of <h1>, <h2>, and <h3> headings and how to organize the main sections and subsections.

I also used AI to help draft paragraphs explaining the documents to prepare, choosing an account type, completing the application, and accessing banking services after opening an account.

## What I Learned

I learned that headings should follow a logical hierarchy. An `<h1>` identifies the main topic, <h2> represents a main section, and <h3> represents a subsection under an <h2>.

I also learned that every heading should have a useful paragraph beneath it and that the outline should make sense to a reader even without reading the paragraphs.
